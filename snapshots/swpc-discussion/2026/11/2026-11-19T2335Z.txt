Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity reached moderate levels (M1-R1) off the WSW limb at
19/1324. C-class reached low levels from Regions 4572 (N11W62,
Cro/beta), 4574(N15E04, Hax/alpha), 4577 (S07W60, Cro/beta), and 4581
(S08E38, Dai/beta) during the past 24 hours.

18/1320 UTC. Region 4576 followed suit producing departing activity in
the form of a C4.9 as well which peaked at 19/0049 UTC. Region 4581
(S09E45, Dai/beta-gamma) remained noteworthy as it grew in length,
gained additional spots and exhibited maturing penumbra on both its
leading and trailing spots. Despite this growth, AR 4581 only produced
C1 and C2 flares during the reporting period.

Other activity included a minor eruption originating from Region 4579
(N15E04, Hax/alpha) which began at approximately 19/0708 UTC as observed
in GOES SUVI 304 imagery. A possibly related subsequent narrow and faint
CME was then seen in LASCO C2 and STEREO-A coronagraph imagery beginning
near 19/0924 UTC and 19/0753 UTC, respectively. Modeling of this event
was attempted with results pending still pending as of the time of this
writing.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be at a chance for M-class flares (R1-R2,
Minor-Moderate) on 19 Nov. Probabilities for M-class events decrease to
a slight chance on 20-22 Nov as Region 4578 fully exits the western
limb.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux reached high levels with a peak
flux of 1,004 pfu observed at one five minute interval at 18/1630 UTC.
The greater than 10 MeV proton flux was at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to reach high levels on
19-21 Nov. The greater than 10 MeV proton flux is expected to continue
at background levels on through 21 Nov.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters reflected relatively weak positive polarity CH HSS
influences. Total fiend generally ranged 3-6 nT. The Bz component of the
IMF underwent a few sustained southward deflections reaching -3 to - 4
nT, but was otherwise near neutral or northward. Solar wind speeds
decreased from ~600 km/s to about 385 km/s by periods end. Phi was
mostly predominantly through the period.

.Forecast...
Sustained positive polarity CH HSS influences are expected to persist
for the beginning of 20 Nov and the potential onset of a weak negative
polarity CH HSS on 21 Nov. This will likely be the cause of additional
disturbances on those respective days.

Geospace
